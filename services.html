Services

Printing, binding, packaging & designing.

With over 60 years of experience (established in 1960), Rajiv Book Binding House specializes in complete print production, custom packaging, and graphic designing solutions.

Request QuoteTalk to Us

Designing Services

We foster an in-house design team that specializes in branding, layout designing, and basically everything graphics to cater to all your print-ready artwork requirements.

Offset Printing

Established in 1960, we specialize in text books, magazines, reports, brochures, catalogues, year books, and booklets from 8 pages to 200+ pages in large quantities with ultra-modern machinery.

Digital Printing

We provide high-quality digital printing with sharp colors, fast turnaround, and precision for both small and bulk orders. Our services are ideal for brochures, flyers, business cards, posters, labels, and customized print materials.

Binding Finishing Options

Our binding department offers diverse finishing options including Saddle Stitched, Perfect Bound (six-clamp), Wiro Bound, center pinning, and Astronic/Polygraph sewing.

Packaging Services

Equipped with top-end machinery for duplex cartons, product boxes, labels, and retail packaging. Complete in-house printing, die-cutting, and box pasting (glueing) serving top clients like Surya Roshni.

Paper & Lamination

Custom paper selection (Matt or Gloss paper depending on preference) with high-grade Thermal Lamination (Matt or Gloss laminated covers).

Large & Small Batches

Experienced staff and state-of-the-art facility equipped for high-volume automated production runs as well as smaller specialized orders. No quantity is too large or too small.
