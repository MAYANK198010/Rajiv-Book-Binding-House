About Us

Almost 60 years of print craft & heritage.

At Rajiv Book Binding House, we are third generation professional binding and printing experts. Hailing from a family in the industry since 1960, we print and bind all types of books and booklets, including: Text Books, Magazines, Reports, Brochures, Catalogues, Year Books, Boxes, etc., in large quantities. So whether it’s an 8-page booklet or a 200-page catalogue, we have the experienced staff and equipment to give you a perfect job.

Branching out further into the Packaging vertical, Rajiv Book Binding House carries the faith entrusted in it by decades of loyal customer base, offering Printing, Die Cutting and Pasting of Boxes with world-standard packaging materials.

Mission

Deliver world-class print and packaging through quality, innovation and continuous state-of-the-art machinery upgrades.

Vision

To be the premier, trusted single-source print production and packaging partner for Indian publishers, corporate brands, and institutions.

Values

Precision execution, complete task ownership, transparent client communication, and reliable, time-bound delivery.

Finishing & Capabilities

Binding & Packaging Department

  • Saddle Stitched, Perfect Bound and Wiro Bound finishing options.
  • Matt or Gloss laminated covers as preferred.
  • Matt or Gloss paper selection tailored to project preferences.
  • Complete packaging workflow: Printing, Die Cutting, and Box Pasting (Glueing).
  • In-house design team for graphics, layout, and branding support.
  • No quantity is too large or too small.
